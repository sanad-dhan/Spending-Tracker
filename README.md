# Spendwise

Your entries stay on your device until you download them.

## Export your monthly report

Click **Download monthly CSV** anytime to download the active month. The report contains:

- monthly income and every expense entry;
- category, group, and description for each expense; and
- budget, spent amount, and money left for every category.

CSV files open directly in Microsoft Excel, Google Sheets, and Apple Numbers. When you click **Start a new month**, Spendwise automatically downloads the completed month, keeps it in month history, and opens the next month.

## Included tracker features

- Saved month history, available from the selector at the top.
- Custom expense dates.
- Budget alerts at 80% and when a category is over budget.
- Wants categories: ChatGPT subscription and Other wants.
- Needs, Wants, and Investments spending charts.
- Optional recurring rent and ChatGPT entries for each new month.
- An all-time investment goal.
- CSV import to restore a previous Spendwise export.

## Publish with GitHub Pages

1. Create a GitHub repository and upload `index.html` and this README.
2. In **Settings → Pages**, select **Deploy from a branch**, then select `main` and `/ (root)`.
3. Open the site and start tracking. Your data remains private in your browser unless you export it.
